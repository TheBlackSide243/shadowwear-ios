ShadowWear 0.10.0: the Ski and snow section. When you add a piece choose the type Ski and snow and tap what it is (suit, goggles, helmet, boots and so on). The ski gear is kept apart: it stays out of the ideas of the day, the change of season and the closet clear-out. In New suitcase there is Snow: with it the suitcase takes all the gear, or you choose it yourself when you pack by hand. Please try adding two pieces of ski gear and making a suitcase with Snow.
