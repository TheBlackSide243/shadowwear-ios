ShadowWear 0.5.3: on the New piece screen, under the photo, there is the "Describe with AI" tick. Turn it on and the AI describes the piece right away: exact kind, brand and size when it reads them, colors and fabric. It stays on for the next pieces. If the AI does not answer, the error is written under it with Try again. Please try adding a piece with the tick off, then turning it on from the piece screen.
